# Evidence Classification System

## Purpose

GEI uses a five-tier evidence classification system to ensure transparency about the nature and strength of each claim.

## Classification Levels

### 1. Textual Evidence
**Definition**: The claim is directly stated in or clearly supported by the biblical text.
**Example**: "The firmament divides the waters above from the waters below" — this is what Genesis 1:7 says.
**Standard**: Any reader can verify this by reading the passage.

### 2. Established Linguistic Evidence
**Definition**: The claim is supported by standard Hebrew scholarship and lexicography.
**Example**: "*Adam* (אָדָם) is related to *adamah* (אֲדָמָה, ground)" — this is recognized in standard Hebrew lexicons.
**Standard**: Can be verified in BDB, HALOT, or other recognized Hebrew reference works.

### 3. GEI Hypothesis
**Definition**: A proposed interpretation developed within the GEI framework.
**Example**: "The firmament represents a dam" — this is GEI's proposed interpretation.
**Standard**: This is what GEI proposes. It is an interpretive claim, not an established fact.

### 4. Engineering Analogy
**Definition**: A comparison between a biblical description and an engineering system or process.
**Example**: "A dam divides water into upper and lower bodies" — this is a verifiable engineering fact used for comparison.
**Standard**: The engineering fact itself must be independently verifiable. The comparison's validity depends on the GEI framework.

### 5. Unverified Research Question
**Definition**: An area where GEI has identified a potential connection but has not developed a full analysis.
**Example**: "Noah's Ark may relate to volume and containment engineering" — this is a research direction, not a developed interpretation.
**Standard**: Marked as requiring further investigation. No conclusions should be drawn.

## Application

Every substantive claim in the GEI knowledge library should carry an evidence classification. This ensures:
- Readers know the nature of each claim
- Hypotheses are not mistaken for established facts
- The framework remains transparent and examinable
- Research gaps are honestly identified
