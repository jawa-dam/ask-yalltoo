# GEI Core Concept Mappings

## Overview

The following table presents the core interpretive mappings proposed by Genesis Engineered Interpretation. Each mapping connects a biblical concept to a proposed hydraulic engineering parallel.

**These are GEI's proposed interpretive associations, not established facts.**

## Mapping Table

| Biblical Concept | GEI Proposed Parallel | Evidence Level |
|-----------------|----------------------|----------------|
| Most High / God | Mountain source or elevation | GEI hypothesis |
| Earth | Dry side of the water-management system | GEI hypothesis |
| Adam | Dam or A-DAM — water containment structure | GEI hypothesis |
| Firmament | Separation and containment structure (dam) | GEI hypothesis |
| Heaven | Upper or water-retaining region | GEI hypothesis |
| Light | Released or flowing water | GEI hypothesis |
| Darkness | Deep or heavy water | GEI hypothesis |
| Whales | Waterwheels | GEI hypothesis |
| Ites (tribal suffixes) | Tributaries | GEI hypothesis |
| Aaron | Manifold comparison | GEI hypothesis |
| Levites | Levee and maintenance comparison | GEI hypothesis |
| Moses | Ram-pump comparison | GEI hypothesis |
| Noah's Ark | Volume and containment investigation | Research question |

## Classification Guide

- **Textual evidence**: The biblical text explicitly states this
- **Established linguistic evidence**: Supported by standard Hebrew scholarship
- **GEI hypothesis**: A proposed interpretation within the GEI framework
- **Engineering analogy**: A comparison with known engineering systems
- **Research question**: An area requiring further investigation

## Important Notes

1. These mappings are proposed within GEI's interpretive framework
2. They should not be confused with established Hebrew definitions
3. Each mapping has varying levels of textual, linguistic, and engineering support
4. Alternative interpretations exist for every concept listed
5. Some mappings (Aaron, Moses, Levites) extend beyond the core Genesis 1–6 scope and are noted as broader GEI research directions
