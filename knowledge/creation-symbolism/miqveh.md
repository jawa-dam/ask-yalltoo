# Miqveh — Ritual Water Collection

## Definition

A **miqveh** (also spelled mikvah, mikveh; Hebrew: מִקְוֶה) is a collection or gathering of water. In Jewish tradition, it refers to a ritual immersion pool used for purification.

## Biblical Usage

The Hebrew word *miqveh* appears in Genesis 1:10:

> "...and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas" (KJV)

The word translated "gathering together" is derived from the root קוה (*qavah*), meaning to collect, gather, or wait.

## GEI Observation

GEI notes that:
- The concept of a *miqveh* is fundamentally about **water collection and containment**
- Genesis 1:10 describes the creation of a collected water body
- In GEI's framework, this collected water body corresponds to a reservoir or millpond
- The ritual significance of the miqveh in later tradition may carry echoes of the water-management concepts present in the creation narrative
